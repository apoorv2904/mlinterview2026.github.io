Eight chapters · primitives → axes → composition → throughputread in order

A practical series on training models that do not fit on one GPU. It starts with the communication primitives everything else is built from, works through each axis of parallelism in turn, then covers composing them and making the result fast.

Every chapter is self-contained and runnable, but they are ordered deliberately — later chapters use earlier ones as vocabulary rather than re-explaining.

📝 If you only have time for three

03, 05, 08. Chapter 03 gives you the vocabulary, 05 is the axis most jobs actually use, and 08 is the one that decides whether your model fits at all. Chapters 06 and 07 matter once a single GPU cannot hold one layer; 09 only once you are running all of them together; 11 once your context gets long.

If your model already fits and you just want it faster, read 10 first and work backwards.

Conventions

  • Blocks marked 📝 Note expand on something the main text assumes — the reasoning behind a design, a derivation, or a number worth internalizing.
  • Blocks marked ⚠️ are traps: things that produce silently wrong results, silently slow results, or hangs. They are the parts most worth reading twice.
  • Dark boxes are rules worth memorizing.
  • Diagrams are ASCII so they survive copy-paste into a terminal, a code review, or a text editor.
